# DESIGN SYSTEM: TEMPATMU PULIH & TEMANI AKU
*An Artisanal, Empathetic Sanctuary for Mental Solace & Recovery*

---

## 1. Cinematic & Artisanal Soul (Mood & Atmosphere)
- **Concept:** *A sunlit corner of an archival wooden library during gentle rain.* 
- **Tone:** Quiet, dignified, grounded, deeply human. An antidote to cold clinical diagnosis and generic corporate wellness apps.
- **Atmospheric Depth:**
  - Textured warm paper & archival linen surfaces.
  - Subtle lighting: Gentle diffuse parchment gradients and twilight depth, rejecting harsh neon glows and generic AI floating spheres/orbs.
  - Micro-hairline borders that feel like hand-pressed bookbinding margins rather than synthetic glassmorphism.

---

---

## 3. Expressive Editorial Typography
- **Display & Headings:** `Lora`, serif.
  - Editorial authority, compassionate literary rhythm, italic quotes that feel spoken from the heart.
  - Leading: `1.35` for display, `1.75` for pull quotes.
- **Body & Controls:** `Inter`, system sans.
  - High legibility, optical sizing, neutral and unobtrusive so the emotion of the content takes center stage.
  - Letter-spacing: `-0.01em` on paragraphs, `+0.04em` on uppercase category eyebrow labels.

---

## 4. Tactile Depth & Craftsmanship
- **No Floating Orbs:** Banned generic blurred pastel balls (`.circle.c1`, `c2`, `c3`). Replaced with warm organic radial atmospheric light and paper-grade noise/texture.
- **Structural Radii Hierarchy:**
  - Cards & Content Containers: `12px` to `16px` (crisp, confident, book-page feel).
  - Controls, Inputs & Tags: `6px` to `8px` (tactile, deliberate, avoids lazy uniform 100px pills everywhere).
  - Toggles & Status Pills: Restrained `20px` radius used strictly where semantic continuity applies.
- **Shadows:** Grounded, layered ambient occlusion (`0 2px 8px -1px rgba(27,51,39,0.06), 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(27,51,39,0.08)`), eliminating hazy mud.

---

## 5. Anti-Slop Content & Interactive Rules
- **No Decorative Emoji Spam:** Removed decorative emoji prefixes in headers, navigation, and badges (e.g. no `🌿`, `✨`, `❌`, `💡`, `✦` noise). Real, meaningful icons or clean typographic cues only.
- **Bespoke Interactive Elements:**
  - Interactive tactile audio synthesizer / ambient dock.
  - Kinetic breathing pacing disc.
  - Clean typographic category filters with physical inset/active indicator.
  - Full-size letter reader that treats community messages like cherished physical stationery.
- **Human Voice:** Direct, unhurried, empathetic Indonesian and English copy without motivational poster clichés or generic AI buzzwords.
